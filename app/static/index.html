Agentic Research Pipeline & Mathematical Foundations

VeriCite AI is engineered around strict epistemological grounding, preventing hallucination through five modular layers.

01

Query Decomposition

Deconstructs complex research queries into up to 3 orthogonal search angles, resolving multi-hop references and comparative entities.

02

Hybrid RRF Retrieval

Combines sparse BM25 token frequencies with dense normalized embeddings via Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF):

RRF(d) = Σ (w_i / (k + rank_i(d)))
03

Relevance Guardrail

Assesses the maximum similarity score against a threshold (θ = 0.18). Unanswerable and out-of-domain queries trigger immediate safe refusal.

04

Anchored Synthesis

Constrains LLM generation to provided source blocks, enforcing granular sentence-level citation tags: [^doc#c001].

05

Anti-Hallucination Audit

Post-generation verification extracts all claims, measuring token recall and 3-gram continuous alignment against cited source passages.
